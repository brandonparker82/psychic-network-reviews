      What the Review Lab measures

      • Specific detailDates, amounts, durations and steps taken make an account easier to test.
      • Checkable claimsA price, a policy or a date that a reader can look up.
      • BalanceHonest accounts usually mention at least one limit or downside.
      • Natural wordingEveryday phrasing instead of slogans and repeated keywords.
      • No sales pressurePromo codes, urgency and links are worth a second look.
      • Story holds togetherA clear sequence without contradictions or sudden jumps.

      Each signal is scored from 1 to 5 and averaged into a score out of 100. The score describes the text, not the person. Short, angry or enthusiastic reviews can be perfectly honest, which is why every result ends with practical checks you can do yourself.       Review Lab questions

      Can the Review Lab tell me if a review is fake?

      No. It reads only the wording you paste and describes signals such as how specific, checkable and balanced the text is. It cannot confirm who wrote a review or whether the purchase happened, so treat the score as a prompt to look closer, not a verdict.

      Who are Inez, Gus and Pia?

      They are three AI voices, not people. Inez counts concrete details, Gus is blunt and sceptical but fair, and Pia reminds you that genuine reviews are often messy. They look at the same signals and phrase the findings differently.

      Why would a real review score low?

      Many honest reviews are short, emotional or written in a hurry, and that can lower a score. A low result means the text gives little to check, not that the author is dishonest.

      Is my pasted text stored?

      The text is sent once to produce the analysis. Please do not paste names, emails, phone numbers or account details.

      What should I do after a mixed result?

      Follow the three checks at the bottom of the result, compare the review with several others on different sites, and read our network reviews for how the platforms describe their own pricing and policies.
